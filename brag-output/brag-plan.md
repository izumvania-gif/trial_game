# /brag — EFERON: The Other Hand

**What it is.** A browser game about a Greek city that lives its last day over and over. A scribe notices someone keeps writing in his chronicle at night, and the handwriting is his own.

**Who it's for.** People who liked Outer Wilds, Majora's Mask and Return of the Obra Dinn: time loops, knowledge-only progress, deduction.

**What sets it apart.** Every stage of the game is a different genre with its own camera and palette: dithered 1-bit marble, a black-figure-vase town, and the sea, the only thing drawn in full colour. The CYCLE RUN counter is shared by every player.

**Spoiler rule.** Nothing past the first hour: no Desk, no operators, no identity twist, no endings, no strikes. Only what the prologue and the first loops show: the hand-written line, the stele, the city on a clock, the carved spiral, déjà vu, midnight and the reset.

**Tone.** `cinematic`: trailer scale, big serif type, dramatic cuts, low drone and the game's own lyre.

**Hook (0–2.8 s).** A black screen, and a line appears in a slanted hand: *Don't look at the sky. Look into the stone.* That is the game's first line.

**Share caption.** "A Greek city lives its last day again and again, and someone keeps writing in your chronicle in your own hand."
